### How this should be positioned — an honest reading

**The evidence is genuine and specific, and specificity is exactly what the About page needed.**
A named gurukula, a named seven-year period, a dated dīkṣānta certificate, and three named
signatories — one of them a widely recognised figure — do more credibility work than any
amount of prose.

**But it must be framed accurately.** The Ācārya (M.A.) is *expected* 2026, not held. Documented
formal teaching is roughly seven months. This is an early-career teacher with unusually rigorous
traditional formation — **not** a teacher with decades behind him, and the site must not imply
otherwise. The audiences ranked highest in V4 (parents entrusting children, international
students with no way to verify locally) are precisely the ones who check, and an inflated claim
would be both wrong and fragile.

**The strong, true positioning:** *formed inside an authentic Gurukula for seven years, formally
credentialed, still in advanced study, and teaching in the very tradition he was shaped by.*
Youth is not a liability when paired with verifiable rigour and a named lineage. Overstatement
is. **Standing rule holds: nothing is claimed that the documents do not support.**

> 🔶 OPEN — **Name discrepancy.** The dīkṣānta certificate reads *Vishweshwara N Mayachara* /
> चि॥ विश्वेश्वरः एन् मयचारः; the resume and profile read *Vishweshwara M*. Certificates the
> platform issues, and any legal page, need one settled legal name and one settled public name.

> 🔶 OPEN — still not supplied: the **individual guru** under whom he studied (the certificate
> names the institution and its officers, not a personal teacher); the **śākhā** of Krishna
> Yajurveda (Taittirīya is implied by the Rudram/Chamakam/Aruna-praśna syllabus but is not
> stated); the **pāṭha level attained** (saṁhitā / pada / krama / jaṭā / ghana); and the
> **Advaita sampradāya**.

### A model for our own certificates

The dīkṣānta certificate is a template worth studying rather than replacing. It is an
**āśaṁsanapatram** — a blessing, not a transcript. It carries a Sanskrit benediction, a
Sanskrit subhāṣita, three signatories, and **a date given in both Gregorian and pañcāṅga form**
(विश्वावसु संवत्सर, चैत्र-शुद्ध-त्रयोदशी, गुरुवासरः). That dual dating answers the earlier open question
about pañcāṅga awareness with a real precedent, and should be adopted.

## Part 2 — The three curricula

All three follow one rhetorical structure: philosophy → staged journey → methodology →
"why this is different." That consistency is a brand asset and should be preserved in the site's
Learning Paths pages.

### Sanskrit — संस्कृताध्ययनपरम्परा

**6 stages · 18–24 months · the only path with stated durations.**
Governing principle: *संस्कृतव्याकरणं विना संस्कृतज्ञानं न सिध्यति.*

| Stage | Name | Focus | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | संस्कृतप्रवेशः | Foundation, phonetics, vocabulary | 2–3 months |
| II | वाक्यनिर्माणम् | Sentence formation | 3–4 months |
| III | शब्दरूपाध्ययनम् | Noun declension | 3–4 months |
| IV | धातुरूपाध्ययनम् | Verb structure | 3–4 months |
| V | व्याकरणप्रवेशः | Structured grammar — sandhi, samāsa, kāraka | 3–4 months |
| VI | शास्त्रसंस्कृतम् | **Laghu Siddhānta Kaumudī**, selected prakaraṇas | 4–5 months |

Reading progression: words ➔ sentences ➔ stories ➔ small ślokas ➔ subhāṣitas ➔ granthas.
Stage I includes **strict Devanagari writing discipline** — a direct platform requirement
(handwriting upload and correction).

### Bhagavad Gītā — भगवद्गीताध्ययनपरम्परा

**5 stages · no durations stated.**

| Stage | Name | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| I | श्लोकप्रवेशः | Pronunciation, recitation discipline, dhyāna ślokas, Guru Stotram |
| II | श्लोककण्ठपाठः | Memorisation — Ch. 12, Ch. 15, selected Ch. 2 and Ch. 16 |
| III | पदच्छेदः एवं शब्दार्थः | Word separation and meaning; core vocabulary |
| IV | तात्पर्यविचारः | Philosophical understanding — the three yogas, dharma |
| V | जीवनोपयोगीगीता | Practical application in modern life |

Advanced path: all 18 chapters · Mahābhārata context · comparative study of the yogas ·
introduction to **Gītā Bhāṣya**.

### Krishna Yajurveda — कृष्णयजुर्वेदाध्ययनपरम्परा

| Stage | Name | Content |

| II | सूक्ताध्ययनम् | Śrī, Medhā, Durgā, Puruṣa, Viṣṇu, Sarasvatī, Devī, Saura, Prātaḥ, Go, Brahma Sūktas |

The Stage IV/V syllabus is **Taittirīya**, though the document never says so — worth stating
explicitly, since it is a precision signal to anyone inside the tradition.

### Gaps against the stated subject list

The founder profile names **five** subjects. Curricula exist for three.

- **Advaita Vedānta** — no curriculum. Which prakaraṇa granthas? Tattvabodha → Vedāntasāra →
  Pañcadaśī → Upadeśasāhasrī? Which commentaries? What sampradāya?
- **Stotras and Sūktas** — no separate curriculum, and it substantially **overlaps Yajurveda
  Stages I–III.** Is it a distinct path for students who want recitation without the full Veda
  commitment, or is it simply the early Yajurveda stages under another name? This matters:
  it is probably the lowest-commitment entry point and therefore the most popular offering.

## Part 3 — 🔴 The critical finding

**All three curricula state that teaching is one-to-one.**

> "personalized one-to-one learning" — *all three documents*
> "Every student progresses individually based on their capability and mastery." — Sanskrit
> "Every student progresses individually. Precision remains the priority." — Yajurveda

This invalidates the cohort/batch model assumed throughout the interview so far, and it collides
directly with the V6 target.

### The arithmetic

| Active students | 1 session/week @ 45 min | @ 30 min |
|---|---|---|
| 75 | **56 hours/week** | 37.5 hours/week |
| 100 | **75 hours/week** | 50 hours/week |

**Teaching time alone**, before homework review, audio feedback, Avalokanam, or administration.
Compounded by the fact that students span India, Europe, the US, and Australia — the slots
cannot be packed contiguously, because they must straddle multiple waking windows.

**75–100 one-to-one students is not achievable by a single Acharya.** One of these must give:

1. **Group sessions** for some stages — plausible: early Sanskrit stages and Stotra recitation
   work well in groups, while Yajurveda pronunciation correction genuinely requires 1:1.
   A **hybrid** model per path or per stage is the likeliest true answer.
2. **Fewer students** — 25–35 one-to-one students is a realistic ceiling for one teacher.
3. **Lower frequency** — fortnightly rather than weekly for some paths.

**This is the single most consequential open question in the project.** It determines the
scheduling model, the Zoom integration (per-student recurring meetings vs. per-batch),
the progress model (individual position vs. cohort position), the Abhyāsa review queue, and
whether "batch" exists in the data model at all. Nothing in the Student Portal or Teacher
Dashboard can be designed until it is settled.

## Part 4 — Other platform requirements extracted

- **"Digital handwritten explanation"** (Sanskrit methodology) — the Acharya writes by hand
  during teaching. Implies annotation/whiteboard capture and a place for those artefacts to live.
- **Devanagari handwriting discipline** (Sanskrit Stage I) — students submit handwritten work;
  photo upload plus annotated correction is a real Abhyāsa requirement.
- **"Continuous assessments"** — Avalokanam is ongoing, not only end-of-stage.
- **Pronunciation correction** appears in every path — audio recording, playback, and audio
  feedback are core, not optional.
- **Progress = position within a stage of a path**, per student. This is the shape of the
  Progress Tracking module.
- Sanskrit has durations; Gītā and Yajurveda do not. Yajurveda Stage V is effectively
  open-ended. Progress cannot be modelled as a percentage of a fixed timeline for all paths.

> 🔶 RECOMMENDATION — the logo is rendered in a **metallic gold gradient with highlights and
> soft shadow.** D-05 refuses gradients precisely because they date; the treatment also
> reproduces poorly when small, in one colour, embossed, or on parchment. The mark's underlying
> *form* — tree, book, circle, star — is strong and worth keeping. Recommend commissioning a
> **flat single-colour redraw** (solid maroon, or solid gold for certificates and seals) as the
> primary web mark, retaining the gradient version only where it is printed large. This is a
> recommendation, not a decision — the Acharya rules.
