## Why I Teach

I teach because I believe Sanskrit is not merely a language—it is the key to understanding
India's timeless knowledge tradition. Through Sanskrit, students gain access to the Vedas,
Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, and the wisdom preserved by generations of Acharyas.

My purpose is to make this knowledge accessible to sincere learners around the world while
preserving its authenticity.

## The Guru–Shishya Tradition

I believe that true learning happens through the Guru–Shishya Paramparā.

Knowledge is not transferred through recorded videos alone. It grows through guidance,
discipline, practice, correction, reflection, and continuous interaction between the teacher
and the student.

Technology should support this relationship, not replace it.

That is why Vishweshwara Sanskrit is built as a Digital Gurukula rather than a conventional
online learning platform.

## My Teaching Method

Every student follows a structured learning journey.

- Live classes with direct guidance
- Regular Abhyāsa (practice)
- Pronunciation correction
- Homework and revision
- Avalokanam (assessment)
- Personal feedback
- Gradual progression through the curriculum

The focus is not on completing lessons quickly but on developing understanding, confidence,
and mastery.

## About the Paramparā

I have received my education through the traditional Gurukula system, where discipline, daily
study, chanting, reflection, and guidance from Acharyas formed the foundation of learning.

This platform carries forward those values in a modern digital environment.
