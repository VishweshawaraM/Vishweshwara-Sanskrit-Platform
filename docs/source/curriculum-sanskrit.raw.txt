Preserving India’s Sacred Knowledge Systems Through Personalized Traditional
Learning
Our Educational Philosophy
Sanskrit is not merely a language. It is the foundational medium through which India preserved
philosophy, Veda, literature, science, logic, ritual traditions, poetry, metaphysics, and spiritual
knowledge for thousands of years.
To truly understand Sanskrit, vocabulary alone is insufficient. True Sanskrit learning demands
structure. It demands discipline. It demands grammar .
Our teaching philosophy is based on one timeless principle:
संस्क ृ तव्याकरणं विना संस्क ृ तज्ञानं न सद्ध्यत। 
(Without Sanskrit grammar , true Sanskrit knowledge cannot arise.)
At Vishweshwara Sanskrit, students do not merely memorize words. Students gradually develop
the ability to understand Sanskrit independently. This is not casual tutoring; this is authentic
Sanskrit knowledge transmission.
Complete Learning Journey
Total Exact Duration: 18 Months (Minimum) to 24 Months (Maximum)
Our Sanskrit learning pathway is structured carefully to guide students from the very first sound
toward the direct understanding of Sanskrit texts. The objective is not short-term memorization,
but independent understanding. The student progresses through six carefully designed stages.

Detailed Curriculum Progression
STAGE I: संस्क ृ तप्रविेशः (Foundation of Sanskrit Learning)
Duration: 2 – 3 Months
The  first  stage  builds  the  strongest  foundation.  Students  begin  understanding  Sanskrit
sounds, vocabulary, writing systems, and pronunciation purity. Students receive repeated
pronunciation correction to ensure phonetic purity.
Pronunciation Science
स्वराः (Vowels)
व्यञ्जनिानि (Consonants)
ह्रस्व / दीर्मा / प्लुत (Short, Long, Extended)
अल्पप्राण / हाप्राण (Aspiration levels)
अनिुस्वारः / वसर्माः / योवाहाः
Alphabet Association
अ = अजः
आ = आम्र्
इ = इक्षुः
उ = उल्लूकः
क = कपोतः
Vocabulary Development & Expression
परचयः (Self-Expression):  निा ______,  वयः ______, अहं ______ निरे वसा।
शरीराङ्गान (Body Parts): निेत्र्, कणर्माः, हस्तः, निासका, ुख्, पादः
भोजनपदाार्थाः (Food): अन्न्, फल्, दुग्ध्, जल्, ृत्
विस्तून (Objects): पुस्तक्, लेखनिी, द्वार्, आसनि्, फलक्
सङ ् ख्याः (Numbers): १ – १०
Writing Practice: Strict Devanagari writing discipline.

STAGE II: विाक्यनार्थाण् (Sentence Formation System)
Duration: 3 – 4 Months
Students  begin  understanding  how  Sanskrit  forms  expression.  Isolated  words  now
transform into connected language.
Core Components
Personal Pronouns: अह्, त्व्, सः, सा, तत्, वय्, आवा् (Examples: अहं छात्रः अस्स्मि। सा बालिका
अस्त।)
लिङ्गान (Grammatical Gender): पुल्लङ्ग् (राः), स्त्रीलङ्ग् (रा), निपुंसकलङ्ग् (फल्)
विचनान (Numbers/Plurality): एकवचनि् (राः), द्ववचनि् (राौ), बहुवचनि् (रााः)
Simple Sentence Formation: अहं च्छा। बालकः पठत। सा फलं खादत। ाता भोजनिं पचत।
प्रश्नविाचकशब्दाः (Question Words): कः, का, क्, क ु त्र, कदा, कथ् (Examples: भवतः नास्मि कस्मि्? त्वं क ु त्र
वसस?)
षष्ठी विभक्तः (Possession):Examples: रास्मिस्य पुस्तकस्मि्। सीतायाः गृहस्मि्।
STAGE III: शब्दरूपाध्ययन् (Noun Transformation System)
Duration: 3 – 4 Months
Students  begin  understanding  how  Sanskrit  words  transform  according  to  their
grammatical function within a sentence.
Primary Shabdarupas: अकारान्त पुल्लङ्ग (रा), आकारान्त स्त्रीलङ्ग (रा), अकारान्त निपुंसकलङ्ग (फल),
इकारान्त (हर), उकारान्त (ुरु), ऋकारान्त (पतृ)
Pronoun Declensions: अस्द्, युष्द्, तत्, क्
Vibhakti System (Cases): प्रथा, द्वतीया, तृतीया, चतुथर्थी, पञ्ची, षष्ठी, सप्ती
Applied Learning: राः वनिं च्छत। रास्य त्र् आच्छत। राेण पत्रं लख्यते।

STAGE IV: धातुरूपाध्ययन् (Verb Structure System)
Duration: 3 – 4 Months
Students  begin  understanding  verb  architecture.  Action  fully  enters  the  language
framework.
Primary Dhatus: पठ्, ्, भू, क ृ , लख्, खाद्, वद्, पा, स्था, निी, क्रीड्, ज्ञा
लिकाराः (Tenses & Moods):
लिट् (Present): पठा, पठस, पठत
लिङ ्  (Past): अपठत्, अच्छत्
लिृट् (Future): पठष्यत, ष्यत
लिोट् (Command): पठतु, च्छतु
Applied Learning: बालकः पठत। बालकः अपठत्। बालकः पठष्यत। बालकः पठतु।
STAGE V: व्याकरणप्रविेशः (Structured Grammar Mastery)
Duration: 3 – 4 Months
Students transition from basic grammar to formal, traditional grammatical study.
विभक्तव्यविस्था (Deep Case Systems): प्रथा to सप्ती applications.
सन्धिप्रकरण् (Word Combinations): स्वरसन्धिः, व्यञ्जनिसन्धिः, वसर्मासन्धिः
सासप्रकरण् (Compound Words): तत्पुरुषः, कर्माधारयः, द्वन् द्वः, बहुव्रीहः
कारकप्रकरण् (Action Syntax): कतार्मा, कर्मा, करण्, सम्प्रदानि्, अपादानि्, अधकरण्

STAGE VI: शास्त्रसंस्क ृ त् (Advanced Traditional Sanskrit)
Duration: 4 – 5 Months
The final stage introduces advanced traditional Sanskrit scholarship, enabling direct access
to original texts.
Primary Text Focus: Laghu Siddhanta Kaumudi
Selected Prakaranas: अजन्तप्रकरण्, हलन्तप्रकरण्, सन्धिप्रकरण्, सासप्रकरण्, कारकप्रकरण्,
क ृ दन्तप्रकरण्, तद्धितप्रकरण्
Applied Reading Journey & Outcomes
Throughout the learning journey, students gradually begin independent reading. Progression
happens organically:
Words ➔ Sentences ➔ Stories ➔ Small Shlokas ➔ Subhashitas ➔
Granthas 
Final Learning Outcome
After completing the Sanskrit learning journey, the student gradually develops the ability to:
Speak simple Sanskrit naturally.
Understand Sanskrit grammar deeply.
Read Sanskrit independently.
Understand simple Sanskrit shlokas and Subhashitas directly.
Understand smaller Sanskrit granthas confidently.
Build a strong, unshakable foundation for advanced scriptural study.
